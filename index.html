About & Limitations

An honest description of DefectIQ Lite v0.1.

What it does

DefectIQ Lite is a browser-based image-inspection assistant for plain cotton handloom fabric. It highlights visually unusual local regions so an inspector can review them.

It is a human-in-the-loop tool: software proposes areas for attention, while a trained inspector makes the final decision.

How the engine works

The browser prototype analyses local pixel contrast and texture deviation. It compares each area with its surrounding neighbourhood, identifies unusually different regions, groups them, and draws candidate boxes.

This is not a trained AI model. No accuracy, precision, recall, or defect-probability claim is made.

What it cannot do

  • Reliably detect every hole, broken weave, missing yarn, stain, or weaving defect.
  • Detect defects hidden by folds, shadows, blur, glare, or poor lighting.
  • Replace tactile inspection or fabric-domain expertise.
  • Confirm whether a highlighted region is truly a defect.

Future improvements

  • Collect consented, labelled fabric images.
  • Build a validated plain-cotton dataset.
  • Add a lightweight PyTorch anomaly-detection model.
  • Measure performance only on a properly separated test dataset.
  • Add defect-type tagging and region-level reporting.
Product status: DefectIQ Lite v0.1 is an early experimental prototype. It is not commercially ready and does not claim validated performance or market adoption.
